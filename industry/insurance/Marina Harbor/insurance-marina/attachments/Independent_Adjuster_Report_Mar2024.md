## Structures Inspected

Fixed dock system and boathouse, scheduled under Endorsement #3 of policy MPP-2011-44873.

## Findings

| Item | Finding |
|---|---|
| Fixed dock | **Total loss.** Piles sheared at the mudline; decking and framing destroyed; utilities torn away. |
| Boathouse | **Total loss.** Structure collapsed off its piles into the basin; nothing salvageable. |
| Cause of loss | Windstorm and wave action from the February 6–7, 2024 nor'easter — a covered peril under Endorsement #3. |
| Pre-existing condition | No evidence of prior deterioration, rot, or deferred maintenance that would materially reduce the loss. |
| Contractor estimate review | Bayside Marine estimate #4471 ($412,600) — scope and unit pricing reasonable for current market. |

## Conclusion

Both scheduled structures are confirmed total losses from a covered peril. The loss is consistent with the reported cause and the contractor's scope. I recommend the claim be adjusted on a replacement-cost basis subject to the endorsement limit.
