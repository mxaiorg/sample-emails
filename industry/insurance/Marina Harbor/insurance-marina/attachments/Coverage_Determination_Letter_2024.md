Meridian Mutual has completed its review of the February 2024 loss to your fixed dock and boathouse and confirms coverage as follows.

## Determination

The damaged structures are scheduled under **Endorsement #3 — Dock & Boathouse Structures**, effective June 15, 2011, insuring the fixed dock and boathouse on a replacement-cost basis at a combined limit of $340,000 against windstorm and wave action. Our independent adjuster has confirmed a total loss from a covered peril. **Coverage is confirmed.**

Note: this endorsement was verified against the original 2011 policy file and correspondence. Although the endorsement was not visible in our current policy system following a 2016 platform migration, the coverage was validly bound in 2011, was previously honored under claim CLM-2011-3320, and remained in force. The loss is covered.
