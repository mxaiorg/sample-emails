## §4 — Preventive maintenance

**4.1** Contractor shall perform preventive maintenance at the frequency stated
in Schedule A for each site. Millbrook (Store 14): **quarterly**, in January,
April, July and October.

**4.2** Preventive maintenance comprises condenser cleaning, filter replacement,
leak inspection, control verification and recording of operating parameters at
each rack. **Discharge-side gauging is not within the scope of a preventive
maintenance visit** and is performed only on a service order.

## §5 — Demand service

**5.1** Contractor shall attend on demand service requests within the response
times stated in Schedule B.

**5.2 A demand service request is opened by Customer through the customer portal
or by written request to Contractor's dispatch function. Contractor shall not be
obliged to attend, and shall not bill against this Agreement, absent an open
service order.**

**5.3** Contractor may open a service order on its own initiative where it
observes a condition requiring attention, in which case Customer shall be
notified before work is performed and may decline, in which event Customer bears
the cost of attendance.

## §6 — Monitoring and reporting

**6.1** Contractor shall maintain remote product-temperature monitoring at each
site equipped for it.

**6.2** Contractor shall transmit a monthly product temperature log to Customer's
nominated facilities contact on the 14th day of each month. **Transmission of a
log is a reporting obligation only. It does not constitute notice to Contractor
of a condition, does not create a service order, and does not oblige Contractor
to review the log's contents.**

## §9 — Limitation

**9.1** Neither party's liability for product loss shall be limited by this
Agreement, the parties having priced the Agreement on that basis.

**9.2** Nothing in this Agreement relieves Customer of its obligation to mitigate.
