## Analyst note

Millbrook frozen shrink is the worst figure in the estate by a wide margin and
**96 % of it sits on a single refrigeration rack** — 91 % on the aisle 12 frozen
line and a further 5 % in the backroom walk-in that the same rack serves. Dry-side shrink at the same
store is 0.9 %, below the chain average, which is not the pattern theft produces —
theft does not confine itself to one aisle and leave the rest of the store clean.

Trailing four quarters at Millbrook: 1.4 %, 2.2 %, 3.3 %, 4.1 %. The trend is
monotonic and began in the FY2020 Q2 period.

**Recommendation: before this is written up as a shrink investigation against
store management, equipment must be ruled out.** I have asked Ledgerwood whether
their service history for the location explains it.
