## Event

Total failure of the compressor rack serving the store's frozen food line and
backroom walk-in freezer, discovered by night crew at approximately 04:00 on
August 18, 2021. Product temperature in aisle 12 recorded at 41 °F and in the
walk-in at 38 °F at discovery. Product-side telemetry places the onset at
approximately 23:40 on August 17; time from compressor failure to that onset is
not establishable, the site having no discharge-side monitoring.

## Quantification

| Component | Basis | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Store frozen inventory, aisle 12 and reach-ins | Physical count, Aug 18–19 | $412,000 |
| **Backroom walk-in — regional hub inventory** | Staged for 11 stores; transfer manifests | **$1,508,000** |
| **Total product loss** | | **$1,920,000** |

The Millbrook backroom walk-in operates as Brightleaf's regional frozen transfer
hub for eleven stores in the north district. Hub inventory at the time of loss
was materially higher than ordinary store-level stock and accounts for 78.5 % of
the product loss.

## Salvage and mitigation

Product moved to refrigerated trailers between 05:30 and 11:00 on August 18.
Salvage value assessed at nil: all recovered product had exceeded the two-hour
threshold above 10 °F and was condemned on food safety grounds rather than
appearance.
