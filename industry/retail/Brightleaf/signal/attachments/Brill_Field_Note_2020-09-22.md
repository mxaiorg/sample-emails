## Finding

Discharge temperature thirty degrees over with normal suction and normal
superheat points at the discharge valve, not the charge and not the metering
device. Valve plate is leaking on the discharge side and the compressor is
reprocessing hot gas.

## Recommendation

Schedule a valve plate replacement on B-3. It will hold as it is for a period but
it will not hold indefinitely, and a discharge valve that fails outright will
take the compressor with it.

This is the failure mode I would expect to produce exactly the intermittent
overnight warming the store has been reporting: the rack recovers during the day
when the load is light and loses ground on the overnight pull-down.

**Not scheduled. No open order on this rack.**
