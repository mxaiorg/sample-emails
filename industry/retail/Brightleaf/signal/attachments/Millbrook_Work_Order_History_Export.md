## Export caveat — READ THIS

Records prior to **January 1, 2022** were migrated from Servicetrack in the
March 2023 platform migration. **The migration carried work order headers,
equipment references, dates and closed amounts. It did not carry technician
notes, attachments or internal correspondence attached to pre-2022 orders.**

The `First Reported` field is populated at order creation from the order open
date. It is not, and has never been, an assertion about when a customer first
raised an issue by any other means.

For WO-109331 the migrated record shows equipment Rack A-1 only. Any observation
a technician made on other equipment during that visit is not recoverable from
this system.
