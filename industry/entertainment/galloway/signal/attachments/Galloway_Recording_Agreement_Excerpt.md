## 3. Term and Album Commitment

3.1 The initial period requires delivery of one (1) studio album (the "Firm Album").

3.2 Company has three (3) separate and consecutive options, each for one (1) further studio album.

3.3 Company exercises an option only by written notice to Artist's manager and Artist's counsel. Each notice is due on the date the parties set in writing for that album. If Company does not give notice by that date, the term ends.

## 5. Delivery

5.1 Each album is due on the delivery date agreed in writing for that album. The parties may move a delivery date by written agreement, including by email between Company's A&R representative and Artist's manager.

## 9. Approvals

9.1 Company shall not license any master recording embodying Artist's performances for use in a commercial, advertisement or brand campaign without Artist's prior written approval in each instance.

9.2 Artist's approvals and refusals under this section are given through Artist's manager.

9.3 Company shall respect any endorsement or category exclusivity Artist has granted to a third party, once Artist's manager has notified Company of it in writing.

## 11. Recording Funds and Tour Support

11.1 Company shall approve a recording fund for each album in writing before recording begins. Additional amounts may be approved in writing by Company's SVP, A&R.

11.2 Any tour support Company agrees to advance is fully recoupable from Artist's royalties.
