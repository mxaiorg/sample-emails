Dean, we disagree, and I want the reason on the record rather than in a meeting.

ASTM F1869 measures emission from roughly the top half inch of slab. On lightweight concrete fill over metal deck it reads low and it reads low reliably, because the surface skin dries long before the body of the fill does. That is precisely the condition on floors 12 through 19. ASTM F2170 in-situ probes read the slab at depth, which is why our specification is written to F2170 and why the 85% threshold is stated in RH and not in pounds.

Fourteen probes at depth read 92 to 96%. Nine surface tests read low. Those results are not in conflict, they are measuring different parts of the same wet slab, and only one of them tells you what the adhesive will see over twenty years.

One more thing. Your 5 pound limit is the carpet tile adhesive. The resilient tile and the heat welded sheet are published at 3 pounds, and five of your nine kits came back at 3.0 or above. Your own data does not clear the products it is being used to clear.

Our recommendation is unchanged. Section 09 05 61 stays.
