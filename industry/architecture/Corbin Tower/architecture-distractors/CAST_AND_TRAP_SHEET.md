## 0. The story these distractors are hard negatives against

Host firm **VAA interiors + architecture** (`vaa-ia.com`), four studios: Culver City (HQ),
Irvine, Iselin NJ, San Francisco.

The signal story: on project **632588 (Culver City series)** — Ardsley Financial Group
Headquarters at Corbin Tower, Levels 12–19 — VAA specified **Section 09 05 61**, a
two-coat epoxy moisture vapor mitigation system, after ASTM F2170 in-situ testing read
92–96% RH against an 85% threshold. The client directed its deletion in writing on
**May 17, 2011** (credit **$347,900**, 18 calendar days), over VAA's written
recommendation of **April 6, 2011**, expressly accepting responsibility. VAA issued
**ASI-031** on May 19, 2011. Flooring failed in 2024; a **$2,150,000** claim was withdrawn
in February 2025 when the archive produced the directive.

**Distractors must never resolve that story.** They exist to look like it and be wrong.

## 4. Format spec — reproduce exactly

```
**Email-1**··
To: Display Name <local@domain>··
Cc: Display Name <local@domain>··
From: Display Name <local@domain>··
Subject: Realistic subject··
Date: March 10, 2011, 09:15··
References:··
Body:··
Body text starts on the next line. Multiple paragraphs allowed.
```

- `··` = **two trailing spaces** on every header field line. Non-negotiable.
- `**Email-N**` sequential from 1, no gaps.
- `Date:` **exactly** `Month DD, YYYY, HH:MM` — full month name, zero-padded day,
  24-hour clock.
- `Cc:` optional. `References:` comma-separated `Email-N`, **backward only**, or empty.
- **Dates must be monotonically non-decreasing by email number across the whole file.**
- No `Attachment:` lines in the distractor file.
- No commas inside display names.
- **American spelling only.** Never "organis-", "recognis-", "analys-", "colour",
  "labour", "licence", "programme", "utilis-", "itemis-", "authoris-", "summaris-".
- Avoid US federal holidays and weekends for send dates.
- Replies must **not** share their parent's minute value.
- Bodies must be substantively distinct from one another — 2 to 5 sentences of real
  professional content each, not template filler.
- Do not use em dashes in body text; use commas or periods.
