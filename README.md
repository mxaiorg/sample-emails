## About

The emails in this repository are generated to test and demonstrate the capabilities of the mxHERO Advanced platform. They are not intended to represent actual emails or individuals.

The repository is composed of collections of emails separated by industry and use-case scenarios. Each scenario describes a story representing an email discovery challenge designed to stress test and benchmark search tools.

You are free to use these emails for your own testing and benchmark purposes.

### mxMCP

mxMCP is a component of the mxHERO Advanced platform designed to process and analyze emails. It is responsible for extracting relevant information from emails and making it available for further processing and analysis. The test results included here are based on mxMCP.

You can learn more about mxMCP and its capabilities at [https://mxhero.com/docs/mxmcp/](https://mxhero.com/ai-to-email-gateway/).
